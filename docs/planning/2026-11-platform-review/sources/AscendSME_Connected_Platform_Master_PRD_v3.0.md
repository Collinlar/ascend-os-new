# AscendSME Connected Platform Master PRD v3.0

**Status:** Authoritative platform baseline  
**Owner:** Bold Vision MultiTech / AscendSME Product  
**Primary market:** Ghana  
**Expansion:** Africa  
**Date:** 16 September 2026  
**Supersedes:** Master PRD v2.0 where v3.0 introduces conflicting commercial, onboarding, catalogue or POS deployment decisions.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

AscendSME is Africa's connected business operating and credibility infrastructure.

Version 3.0 formalises a major product and commercial shift:

> **AscendSME should be easy to start for free and should earn when it saves a business time, gives it better infrastructure, helps it transact, helps it grow or helps it prove itself.**

The most important change is the role of Ascend POS. Ascend POS becomes the primary acquisition and operating entry point for physical businesses. The software is free to install and use on supported mobile devices. Businesses may start manually without paying. Monetization begins when a business chooses accelerated setup, assisted implementation, hardware, integrated payments, growth products, operational products or credibility services.

The same shared platform powers POS, Shop, Services, Office, Documents, Discover and Readiness.

---

## 2. Platform Thesis

AscendSME follows one commercial principle:

> **Start free. Pay when AscendSME creates identifiable value.**

Five monetizable moments define the commercial system:

1. **Get Me Started**: AI setup, assisted setup, migration and catalogue creation.
2. **Give Me Better Infrastructure**: printers, Smart POS, Counter, additional devices, Office and multi-location capability.
3. **Help Me Get Paid**: integrated payment rails and transaction-linked services.
4. **Help Me Get Customers**: Shop growth, Discover, boosts, campaigns and promotion.
5. **Help Me Prove My Business**: verification, reports, readiness and institutional opportunity support.

Recurring infrastructure may be packaged through Assurance, but compulsory monthly software rent is not the primary commercial model.

## 7. POS Deployment Family

| Deployment | Description | Commercial role |
|---|---|---|
| Ascend POS | Free app on supported smartphone | Acquisition |
| Ascend POS Print Kit | Business phone + portable printer + optional setup | Entry paid infrastructure |
| Ascend Smart POS | Dedicated Android terminal with integrated printer | Flagship managed deployment |
| Ascend POS Counter | Larger screen and printer with optional peripherals | Premium fixed-counter deployment |

A business must move between deployment types without migrating business data.

## 8. Catalogue and Smart Setup

The catalogue is a shared platform capability. A product created for POS may be used in Shop, Discover, Documents, inventory and relevant Office workflows.

### Manual setup

Free. Businesses can create products, prices, images, categories and opening stock manually.

### Smart Setup

Paid AI-assisted acceleration that may suggest:

- Product title
- Description
- Category
- Attributes
- Variants
- Spreadsheet cleanup
- Duplicate detection

Prices and stock require explicit merchant confirmation.

### Assisted Setup

Ascend or an approved partner may create the business profile, capture product information, structure the catalogue, configure receipts and payments, connect hardware, configure staff and complete a test sale.

## 10. Discover v3 Role

Discover is a main growth revenue engine. Eligible shared catalogue items may receive organic visibility or paid promotion. Promotion spend must remain separate from trust, verification and readiness.

---

## 11. Services v3 Role

Services remains the primary operating entry point for businesses selling time, skill or expertise. It may cross-sell payments, Documents, Office, Discover and Readiness.

---

## 12. Readiness and Sustainability Score

Sustainability Score remains separate from MOT, Trust Level, Funding Readiness and Evidence Confidence.

Paid product usage shall not automatically improve score. Missing evidence may reduce Evidence Confidence rather than automatically imply poor business performance.
