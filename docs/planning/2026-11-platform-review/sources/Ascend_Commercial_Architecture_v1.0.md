# AscendSME Commercial Architecture v1.0

**Status:** Commercial product architecture baseline  
**Date:** 16 September 2026

---

## 1. Commercial Thesis

AscendSME should not depend on compulsory monthly software subscriptions as its primary revenue model.

> **Start free. Pay when Ascend helps you start faster, operate better, transact, grow or prove your business.**

---

## 3. Free Foundation

Recommended free foundation:

- Ascend POS app
- Manual catalogue
- Basic Shop
- Basic Services
- Basic customer records
- Basic digital receipts
- Basic invoices where appropriate
- Basic evidence history
- Manual payment recording
- Basic organic Discover eligibility
- Basic staff essentials required by anchor products

---

## 4. Setup Revenue

Smart Setup may be sold by AI processing quantity. Assisted Setup may be sold by complexity, catalogue size, photography requirement, staff count, locations, hardware and training.

---

## 5. Hardware Revenue

Revenue can come from:

- Printer margin
- Smart POS margin
- Counter equipment
- Peripherals
- Installation
- Setup
- Accessories
- Assurance

---

## 6. Assurance

Assurance may include managed updates, cloud sync support, secure backup, priority support, device management, hardware support, remote diagnostics and selected operational monitoring.

Historical data access must remain after expiry.

---

## 7. Ascend Balance

Ascend Balance is a transparent prepaid local-currency balance.

Use cases:

- Smart Setup AI processing
- Discover promotion
- SMS
- WhatsApp
- e-signatures
- automation
- verification
- reports

Principles:

- GHS 1 Balance should equal GHS 1 purchasing value.
- Avoid artificial points.
- Avoid short-expiry pressure.
- Keep sponsor-funded credits separate.

---

## 8. Office Commercial Model

Office Essentials remain embedded where necessary. Full Office may use Team Operations Passes.

Illustrative Ghana pilot:

- Up to 5 staff: GHS 600/year
- 6-15 staff: GHS 1,200/year
- 16-30 staff: GHS 2,400/year
- 30+: custom

Alternative periods may include 90 days, six months and 12 months.

---

## 9. Discover Commercial Model

Illustrative Ghana pilot:

- Product Boost: GHS 35 / 7 days
- Service Boost: GHS 35 / 7 days
- Shop Boost: GHS 100 / 14 days
- Location Boost: GHS 60 / 7 days
- Spotlight: GHS 250-350
- Commercial Campaign: GHS 500+

Promotion shall not imply verification.

---

## 10. Documents Commercial Model

Basic documents remain accessible. Paid value may include branding, advanced templates, signatures, recurring documents, reminders, statements, accountant access and verified reports.

## 12. Revenue Mix Target

Long-term working range:

- Setup and implementation: 15-25%
- Hardware and Assurance: 15-25%
- Payments: 15-30%
- Discover and growth: 10-20%
- Office and operations: 10-20%
- Verification, readiness and institutions: 10-25%

This is a portfolio target, not a promise that all streams will mature at the same rate.

---

## 13. Final Definition

> **AscendSME earns from removing friction, providing infrastructure, enabling transactions, driving growth and proving business credibility.**
