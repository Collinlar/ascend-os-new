## 1. Executive Summary

Ascend Discover is the customer discovery and merchant growth layer of AscendSME.

It helps customers find credible businesses, products and services operating through AscendSME, while helping merchants reach customers beyond their existing WhatsApp contacts, social media followers and physical locations.

Ascend Discover is not a fully managed marketplace at launch.

AscendSME does not initially become the seller of record, own merchant inventory, manage every delivery, guarantee every transaction or take control of the customer relationship. The merchant remains responsible for pricing, stock, fulfilment, service delivery, returns and customer support.

Ascend Discover provides:

- Organic business, product and service discovery
- Search and location-based discovery
- Curated collections
- Merchant eligibility and quality controls
- Paid product, Shop, service and business promotion
- Campaign creation and Ascend Balance payments
- Campaign attribution and performance reporting
- SEO and social distribution support
- Customer saving, following and revisiting
- Trust, verification and sponsored-label separation
- Evidence generation from measurable growth activity

The core product promise is:

> **Help more customers find the right AscendSME businesses, products and services while giving merchants a clear, measurable way to grow.**

---

## 2. Product Positioning

Ascend Discover should be positioned as:

> **The discovery and growth layer for businesses operating through AscendSME.**

It should not be positioned as:

- A managed marketplace
- A logistics company
- A universal checkout controlled by AscendSME
- A merchant-ranking system based only on payment
- A public credit score
- A directory containing unverified scraped businesses
- A replacement for the merchant's own Shop, website or customer relationship
- A pay-to-win trust system

Ascend Discover strengthens the wider AscendSME proposition:

> **Run the business through AscendSME. Reach customers through Discover. Build credibility through real activity.**

## 3. Problem Statement

Many African SMEs depend heavily on:

- WhatsApp status
- Instagram and Facebook posts
- Referrals
- Walk-in traffic
- Personal contacts
- Informal online directories
- Repeated manual promotion

These channels can generate interest, but businesses often struggle to:

- Reach people outside their existing audience
- Convert attention into Shop visits, orders or bookings
- Measure what promotion produced
- Build a discoverable business presence
- Distinguish paid promotion from trust
- Maintain consistent product and service information
- Present updated stock, pricing and availability
- Build searchable business records over time

Customers also struggle to:

- Find relevant local businesses
- Compare products and services
- know whether business information is current
- understand whether a result is organic or sponsored
- move from discovery to a trusted merchant-owned Shop or booking page
- save and revisit businesses across different channels

---

## 4. Product Thesis

Ascend Discover is built on five principles.

1. **Discovery must lead to action.**  
   A customer should move from search or collection to a merchant-owned Shop, service page, booking flow, contact action or payment path.

2. **Organic discovery must remain meaningful.**  
   Merchants should be able to qualify for free discovery without being forced to pay.

3. **Paid promotion must be clear.**  
   Sponsored placement must be visibly labelled and must not be confused with verification, trust or editorial selection.

4. **Merchant ownership must remain intact.**  
   The merchant owns the customer relationship, fulfilment and operating records.

5. **Growth activity should become evidence.**  
   Visits, enquiries, orders, bookings and repeat customer actions should feed the Business Evidence Ledger according to their verification level.

## 5. Goals

### 5.1 Business goals

- Create a scalable growth revenue stream for AscendSME.
- Improve Shop, Services and POS merchant acquisition and retention.
- Increase merchant willingness to complete accurate business profiles.
- Increase measurable customer visits, enquiries, orders and bookings.
- Build a cross-business customer discovery destination.
- Support institutional and sponsored campaigns.
- Create a strong Ghana-first customer network that can localise across African markets.
- Achieve a first-year target of 40 percent of eligible Shop businesses purchasing at least one Discover promotion.

### 5.2 Merchant goals

- Reach customers beyond existing personal networks.
- Promote products, services, Shops and businesses when needed.
- Understand what promotion produced.
- Pay through a simple prepaid Ascend Balance.
- Build a stronger organic presence through accurate information and reliable activity.
- Avoid paying for visibility that cannot be measured.

### 5.3 Customer goals

- Find relevant products, services and businesses.
- Search by need, category and location.
- understand whether a result is organic or sponsored.
- Open merchant-owned pages without unnecessary friction.
- Save businesses, products and services.
- Receive relevant recommendations without intrusive advertising.

### 5.4 Platform goals

- Reuse shared customer, catalogue, merchant, location, payment and evidence capabilities.
- Avoid creating separate merchant profiles or customer identities.
- Keep promotion, trust, readiness and verification logically separate.
- Generate standard events for the shared Business Evidence Ledger.
- Support Business Web, Business Mobile, Customer Web and Customer Mobile from one platform core.

---

## 6. Non-Goals

Ascend Discover v2.0 will not initially:

- Become the merchant of record
- Hold merchant inventory
- Manage fulfilment for all merchants
- Guarantee all merchant products or services
- Take responsibility for every return or refund
- Offer a universal multi-merchant cart
- Force customers to create an account before viewing a merchant page
- Publicly expose private readiness, financial or staff data
- Let merchants buy verification, trust badges or better readiness scores
- Rank solely by ad spend
- Promise guaranteed sales from promotion
- Build a separate customer database outside the shared Customer Core
- Build a separate product catalogue outside Ascend Shop or shared Catalogue Core
- Replace merchant customer support

## 11. Merchant Eligibility

A merchant must meet a minimum quality threshold before appearing in organic or paid Discover surfaces.

Minimum eligibility should consider:

- Active AscendSME business
- Valid business owner or authorised manager
- At least one active product or service
- Current pricing or price guidance
- Current location or service area
- Valid customer contact path
- Acceptable media quality
- No unresolved severe policy violation
- Clear fulfilment or booking method
- Required business-category information
- Merchant acceptance of Discover terms

Eligibility should not require:

- A paid subscription
- A high Sustainability Score
- A financing relationship
- A specific number of transactions
- A paid promotion
- Full public disclosure of readiness information

---

## 12. Organic Discovery

Organic discovery is the free placement of eligible businesses, products and services.

Organic ranking may consider:

- Search relevance
- Location relevance
- Listing completeness
- Information freshness
- Stock or availability
- Customer engagement
- Order or booking completion
- Merchant responsiveness
- Customer complaints
- Content quality
- Repeat customer activity
- Policy compliance
- Evidence confidence
- Diversity and rotation

Organic ranking must not:

- Be sold directly
- Be automatically improved by buying unrelated Ascend products
- Expose private score components
- suppress all non-paying merchants
- use misleading scarcity or popularity claims
- permanently favour early merchants

## 16. Core Merchant Journeys

### 16.1 Organic listing journey

1. Merchant activates Shop, Services or a business profile.
2. AscendSME evaluates Discover eligibility.
3. Merchant reviews listing-quality guidance.
4. Merchant accepts Discover terms.
5. Eligible content becomes organically discoverable.
6. Merchant receives performance summaries and quality recommendations.
7. Activity from customers flows into shared Shop, Services, Customer and Evidence records.

### 16.2 Product Boost journey

1. Merchant opens an eligible product.
2. Merchant selects **Promote with Ascend**.
3. Merchant selects Product Boost.
4. Merchant reviews duration, placement and price.
5. Merchant tops up Ascend Balance where required.
6. Merchant confirms the campaign.
7. The system deducts Balance once.
8. Campaign begins after eligibility validation.
9. Sponsored labels appear on every paid placement.
10. Merchant views impressions, clicks, Shop visits and attributed orders.
11. Campaign completes automatically.
12. Merchant receives a concise performance summary.

### 16.3 Customer discovery journey

1. Customer opens Discover through web, mobile or a shared link.
2. Customer searches or browses a collection.
3. Customer sees clearly separated organic and sponsored results.
4. Customer opens a product, service or business.
5. Customer moves to the merchant-owned Shop, booking or contact flow.
6. The merchant receives the resulting customer or transaction record through shared platform capabilities.
7. Discover receives privacy-safe attribution events.

### 16.4 Sponsored programme journey

1. Institution creates a sponsored programme with AscendSME.
2. Merchant cohort is identified.
3. Merchants consent to participation.
4. Programme credit is allocated.
5. Merchants create approved campaigns.
6. Discover delivers sponsored placements with programme labelling.
7. Institution receives approved cohort reporting.
8. Merchants retain customer relationships and operating records.
9. Merchants may continue with self-funded Ascend Balance after programme end.

## 17. Customer Experience Principles

- Browsing should not require account creation.
- Sponsored content must be obvious without being visually disruptive.
- Search should prioritise relevance and useful action.
- Customer Web is the primary low-friction acquisition surface.
- Customer Mobile adds persistence, saving, following and cross-business history.
- Discover should lead customers to merchant-owned conversion flows.
- Public trust indicators must be understandable.
- Customer data should not become an advertising asset sold to merchants.
- Recommendations should remain useful, diverse and controllable.
- Customers should be able to report harmful or misleading content.

## 18. Merchant Experience Principles

- Promotion should be understandable in local currency.
- The merchant should know what is being purchased.
- Campaign creation should take only a few steps.
- Ascend Balance should simplify rather than obscure payment.
- Organic listing should remain useful.
- Reports should distinguish activity from proven outcomes.
- AscendSME should not promise guaranteed sales.

- Business Mobile should support fast action.
- Business Web should support detailed analysis and administration.

## 22. Key Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Discover appears to be a marketplace | Keep merchant ownership, responsibility and conversion flows explicit |
| Paid merchants dominate results | Preserve organic inventory, caps, rotation and clear sponsored labels |
| Merchants confuse promotion with trust | Use separate labels, policies and interfaces |
| Campaigns do not produce measurable value | Improve targeting, attribution, quality guidance and reporting |
| Low-quality listings reduce customer trust | Eligibility, freshness checks, moderation and quality recommendations |
| Fraudulent clicks distort reports | Traffic validation, exclusion rules and anomaly monitoring |
| Customer app adoption is weak | Keep Customer Web as the default and make Mobile optional |
| Ascend Balance feels like artificial credits | Maintain one-to-one local-currency value |
| Too many promotion products confuse merchants | Launch with a small set of clear products |
| Merchant disputes become Ascend liability | Clear merchant responsibility, support routing and policy enforcement |
| Institutions seek excessive merchant data | Consent, cohort-level reporting and access controls |
| Promotions improve scores unfairly | Separate payment from evidence quality and score governance |
| Search favours large merchants | Diversity, rotation and fairness monitoring |
| Outdated products frustrate customers | Inventory and availability sync plus freshness controls |
| Cross-country expansion fragments the product | Shared core with local configuration, not separate codebases |

## 23. Acceptance Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Organic Shop discovery

Given an eligible Shop with ten active products, when the merchant accepts Discover terms, then the Shop and eligible products can appear organically without payment.

### Scenario 2: Product Boost purchase

Given an eligible product and sufficient Ascend Balance, when the merchant purchases a seven-day Product Boost, then Balance is deducted once, the campaign is created and sponsored placement is visibly labelled.

### Scenario 3: Insufficient Balance

Given insufficient Ascend Balance, when the merchant confirms a campaign, then the system offers a top-up flow and does not create or charge the campaign prematurely.

### Scenario 4: Product becomes unavailable

Given an active Product Boost, when shared inventory marks the product unavailable, then promotion delivery pauses and the merchant is notified.

### Scenario 5: Customer web browsing

Given a customer without an account, when the customer opens Discover Web, then the customer can search, browse and open merchant pages without forced registration.

### Scenario 6: Sponsored label separation

Given a sponsored product from an unverified merchant, when shown in Discover, then it displays Sponsored but not Verified or Trusted.

### Scenario 7: Merchant is verified

Given a verified merchant running a paid campaign, when displayed, then Sponsored and Verified appear as separate labels with separate explanations.

### Scenario 8: Attributed Shop order

Given a customer clicks a promoted product and completes a connected Shop order within the attribution window, then the campaign records the eligible attributed order without duplicating the transaction.

### Scenario 9: Refunded order

Given an attributed order is later refunded, when the refund completes, then campaign revenue attribution updates and the Evidence Ledger receives the appropriate reversal.

### Scenario 10: Sponsored programme

Given a bank-funded merchant cohort, when programme credit is issued, then merchants can spend only on approved campaign types and the bank sees approved cohort reporting only.

### Scenario 11: Policy violation

Given a merchant repeatedly uploads a prohibited product, when enforcement occurs, then the item is removed, promotion stops, the action is audited and the merchant receives the appropriate notice.

### Scenario 12: Business Mobile promotion

Given an eligible merchant using Ascend Business Mobile, when the merchant taps Boost on a product, then the merchant can review price, Balance and campaign summary without opening Business Web.

### Scenario 13: Customer follows business

Given a signed-in customer, when the customer follows a business, then the follow synchronises across web and mobile but does not automatically opt the customer into all marketing.

### Scenario 14: Organic ranking fairness

Given a category with paying and non-paying merchants, when a customer browses organic results, then eligible non-paying merchants remain available and sponsored placements remain visibly distinct.

### Scenario 15: Merchant appeal

Given an eligibility rejection, when the merchant requests review, then the system records the appeal, preserves the original reason and routes the case to an authorised operator.

### Scenario 16: Expired campaign

Given a campaign reaches its end date, when the expiry process runs, then paid delivery stops, history remains available and no automatic renewal occurs.

### Scenario 17: Ascend failure

Given a paid campaign cannot run because of an AscendSME platform failure, when the incident is confirmed, then the merchant receives an appropriate Balance refund or campaign extension with audit history.

### Scenario 18: Service discovery

Given an eligible service provider, when a customer opens the service listing, then the customer can view service details and continue into the merchant-owned booking flow.

### Scenario 19: Private readiness data

Given a customer opens a merchant profile, then private MOT, financial and readiness details are not shown.

### Scenario 20: Customer complaint

Given a customer reports serious misrepresentation, when the complaint is submitted, then it enters a moderation workflow without publicly publishing an unverified accusation.

## 25. Final Product Definition

Ascend Discover is:

> **The customer discovery and merchant growth layer of the AscendSME Connected Platform.**

It gives customers a clear way to find relevant businesses, products and services.

It gives merchants a natural way to pay for reach when the value is clear.

It gives AscendSME a scalable monetization layer connected to Shop, Services, Customer Web, Customer Mobile, Ascend Balance and the Business Evidence Ledger.

The product must preserve four boundaries:

1. The merchant remains the seller or service provider.
2. Organic discovery remains meaningful.
3. Paid promotion remains visibly separate from trust and verification.
4. Real customer and transaction outcomes, not promotion spending, determine business evidence quality.

The final promise is:

> **Get discovered. Convert more customers. Keep every result connected to the business you are building.**
