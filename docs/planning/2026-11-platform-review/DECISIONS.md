## Decision procedure

Start with D01–D07, D08–D09 and the correctness policies D22–D23. Engineering can begin the disposable test harness, source-to-runtime inventory and regression reproduction while product decisions are pending. Hardware feasibility can also begin without purchasing or certifying a device.

For each accepted decision record date, approver, rationale, affected source IDs, effective release and migration implications. Keep original source text. Update the requirement assessment to “superseded by Dxx” or “deferred to release X” only after acceptance. A newer source that adds detail does not automatically supersede all prior product behavior.
